Getting started

From opening the app to your new desktop in five steps. Nothing on your desktop changes until you press Apply theme.

Open Plasmify

Open Plasmify from the application menu (it is in the Settings category), or type plasmify gui in a terminal.

If a red banner appears at the top of the window, Plasmify found a problem with your system, for example that you are not in a Plasma 6 session or that a required Plasma tool is missing. The banner says what is wrong, and Apply theme stays disabled until it is fixed.

The main window

  • On the left, the gallery. Each card shows a small preview, the preset's name and its colours. ● Active marks the preset you are using now. Your own presets start with (yours), so a shared preset can never pass itself off as a built-in one.
  • On the right, the selected preset. A large live preview, the description, colour swatches, tags and whether it is a built-in preset or yours. Below them are two tabs: What to apply and Customize.
  • At the bottom, a summary of what will be installed, and the buttons Delete (your own presets only), Save as my preset…, Share…, Credits, Restore… and Apply theme. While the Customize tab holds changes you have not applied yet, a dark bar with Reset and Apply theme floats just above them, and the Apply theme button down here steps aside (see Customize).

1. Pick a preset

Click a card in the gallery. The preview, colours and description update right away. Browse as much as you like: nothing changes on your desktop yet, and changes you make in the Customize tab are kept for each preset while you look at others (until you close the app).

2. Choose what to apply

The What to apply tab lists the parts of a theme as steps you can tick off. Each one has a short description; hover over it for details. Untick anything you want to keep as it is. Parts a preset does not include are greyed out.

StepWhat it changes

WallpapersWallpapers from the projects and collections that publish them, on the desktop and the lock screen, brought to life with Shimmer if the preset has it

A few examples:

  • Keep your panels but take the colours: untick Panel layout and Panel style.
  • Only the terminal look: untick everything except Terminal and Shell prompt.
  • Keep your wallpaper: untick Wallpapers.

The summary at the bottom follows your choices, for example Installs 2 packages (6.1 MB): starship, zoxide or Nothing new to install. It always ends with No background services, no AUR or added repositories. While it says Checking what needs to be installed…, the Apply button waits.

3. Fine-tune it (optional)

The Customize tab lets you change almost anything about the preset: panels, glass, window effects, wallpapers and Shimmer, splash screen, colours, icons, fonts, the terminal and the login screen. The preview follows every change, and the tab title shows how many changes you made, such as Customize (3). As long as something differs from what is applied, a bar at the bottom offers Reset and Apply theme. See Customize.

4. Apply

Press Apply theme. If the preset downloads from a server outside Plasmify's trusted list, you are asked first (see Sharing presets). Then a window shows the progress and a log:

  1. Backup. The settings files the selected steps will change are saved first.
  2. Packages. Missing packages are installed from your distribution's repositories. You may be asked for your password once, through the normal system prompt.
  3. Downloads. Themes, cursors and wallpapers are downloaded from where they are published, never re-hosted.
  4. Each step runs. Plasma may reload once, so your panels can disappear for a moment.
  5. Done. The window says so, lists notes about anything that was skipped, and shows the backup it made.

The progress window cannot be closed half-way, so an apply never stops in the middle. If one step fails, the others still run and the problem is listed as a note.

After a successful apply, the Customize changes you applied are remembered for this preset. They are shown again next time (also after a restart), and the next Apply builds on them.

5. Restore

Not happy? Press Restore… (or use File > Restore a backup…). Every apply saved the settings from before it; pick one by its date and press Restore. The settings Plasmify changed go back to how they were at that moment. Packages it installed and the themes, wallpapers and fonts it downloaded stay. If the restore includes panels or the wallpaper, Plasma reloads once, so the panels briefly disappear.

The very first backup, Your setup before Plasmify, is always kept. Details: Backups and restore.

The live preview

The picture at the top right is a miniature desktop drawn from the preset itself: its palette, its panels with their corners and opacity, windows and a terminal. It is drawn, not a screenshot, so it follows the preset's own settings and needs no download. If the preset's first wallpaper is already on your computer (because the preset ships it, or you applied the preset before), the preview uses it as the background; otherwise it uses the palette's colours.

The preview follows every change in the Customize tab: move a panel to another edge, change the accent or the corner radius, and you see it immediately.
