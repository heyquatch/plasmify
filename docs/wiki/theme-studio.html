Theme studio

Turn a picture you love into a complete theme, and watch it come together in a large live preview.

Open it with File > Create from wallpaper… (Ctrl+N). The studio has four numbered steps on the left, a large preview on the right that follows your colours, panels, corners and opacity, and a strip showing the full palette you are building. Finished steps can be clicked to go back; Back and Next move one step at a time.

Everything stays on your computer

Your pictures are analysed locally. Nothing is uploaded, and the studio itself downloads nothing.

1. Wallpaper

  • Drop pictures on the big box, or click it to choose them: one picture, or several for a slideshow. Up to 30 pictures, PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF.
  • Use my current wallpaper takes the picture that is on your desktop now (the button is disabled if your current wallpaper is not a plain picture).
  • Start over (or a right-click on the box) empties it.

2. Colours

  • Dark or Light: dark backgrounds with light text, or light backgrounds with dark text.
  • Accent, taken from your picture: Plasmify offers six colours built from the hues in your picture and adjusted to work as an accent. A muted picture also gets hues that go with its main colour. Click the one you like.
  • Colourfulness: from 40 to 130 %. Lower is calmer and greyer, higher is more colourful.
  • Text contrast grade: the studio checks how readable the text is as you go, for example Text contrast 9.1:1 (excellent; 7:1 or more is excellent). Grades are excellent (7:1 or more), good (4.5:1 or more) or low.

3. Style

  • Panels: picture tiles for six layouts: Bar + dock, Classic, Top bar, Side dock, Islands and Just a dock.
  • Corners: 0 to 14 pixels of panel corner radius.
  • Opacity: 40 to 100 %; lower lets the wallpaper shine through the panels.
  • Motion: Calm (gentle fades and a soft blur), Lively (windows zoom open and minimise with the magic lamp) or Playful (everything Lively does, plus wobbly windows).
  • Fonts: Inter + JetBrains Mono, Noto Sans + Hack, Roboto + Fira Code, Nunito + Roboto Mono, or Ubuntu + Ubuntu Mono. Missing fonts are installed from your distribution when you apply; fonts it does not package are downloaded for your user only.
  • Surprise me rolls the accent, panels, corners, opacity, motion, fonts and colourfulness. Click as often as you like.

4. Finish

  • Name (required, up to 60 characters) and an optional description.
  • Matching splash screen while Plasma starts: your wallpaper, your accent and a progress bar while you log in.
  • Apply it right away: applies the new theme as soon as it is created.
  • A summary lists what you get: the wallpapers, the palette and accent, the panels and corners, the motion, the fonts, plus Papirus icons with matching folder colours and a themed terminal and prompt.

Press Create theme. The theme is saved as your own preset and appears in the gallery with (yours) in front of its name. Your pictures are stored with it. If you ticked Apply it right away, the usual apply window opens, with a backup first.

Afterwards

  • Fine-tune every detail in the Customize tab, like any other preset.
  • Flip it between dark and light with the Dark / Light switch.
  • Share it: your pictures travel inside the .plasmify file.
  • Delete it from the gallery with Delete when you no longer want it.
