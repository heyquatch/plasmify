Wallpapers

Use the preset's wallpapers, your own pictures, or a mix, in exactly the order you want. Bring them to life with Shimmer, or use pictures that already move.

Your slideshow at a glance

Open the Customize tab and click Wallpapers. The section lists every wallpaper of the slideshow as a thumbnail, numbered in the order they will be shown. Hover over a thumbnail to see its number and credit; pictures that are not on your computer yet say Downloaded when you apply.

  • Reorder: drag a thumbnail to another place.
  • Remove: select one or more thumbnails and press Delete, or click Remove.
  • Add your own: drop picture files onto the list (from Dolphin, for example), or click Add pictures… to choose them. They are added after the pictures already there. PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF work, up to 30 pictures in total.
  • Start over: Preset's list brings back the preset's own wallpapers.

A short note under the list says how many pictures you have. The first picture is also used for the lock screen. If you remove every picture, your current wallpaper simply stays as it is.

Your pictures are copied into Plasmify's own folder (~/.local/share/plasmify/custom), so moving or deleting the originals later does no harm.

One picture or a slideshow

  • Several pictures make a slideshow. Change every sets how often it moves on (every 5 minutes, 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour or 3 hours), and Order shows the pictures As listed or mixed up with Shuffle.
  • One picture is simply your wallpaper. There is nothing to rotate, so Change every (and Order) are disabled; the tooltip explains why. Add more pictures to turn the slideshow back on.

While the section is closed, its summary shows what you have, for example 6 images, every 30 minutes or 1 image.

Living wallpapers (Shimmer)

Shimmer turns still wallpapers into living ones: sparkles, embers, sakura petals, snow, fireflies, bokeh, shooting stars, rain, a turning magic circle or speed lines over your pictures, with a light that plays across them and a slow zoom or pan. The effects favour the edges and corners, so the subject in the middle of the picture stays clear. Pick a style in the Shimmer box under the slideshow; the settings and the live preview are described on the Customize page.

  • Made on your computer. When you apply, Plasmify draws each picture as a seamless six-second loop, at 12 frames per second or 24 if you pick the smoother Frame rate, and saves it as an animated WebP file, which Plasma's own wallpaper plays. That takes a few seconds per picture (twice as long at 24), and nothing is downloaded for it.
  • The first six pictures. A long slideshow brings only its first six pictures to life; the others still take their turn, as still pictures. Reorder the thumbnails to choose which ones move.
  • Made once. The living pictures are kept, so applying again with the same settings is quick. Change the style, colour, light, motion, particles or frame rate and new ones are made; old ones are deleted. Switch Shimmer off and the folder is removed.
  • The lock screen keeps the still picture. So do the splash and login screens.
  • Pictures that already move (GIF and animated WebP) are used as they are.
  • Paused under a full-screen window. Plasma pauses moving wallpapers while a window fills the screen, which saves processor time and battery. Tick Keep moving while windows are open to keep them going.

Shimmer needs only PyQt6, which Plasmify runs on anyway, with Qt's image formats plugin for WebP. Most Plasma desktops already have it; without it, the apply window says so and the wallpapers stay still. Several presets come with Shimmer switched on, such as Catppuccin Anime (sakura petals), Synthwave Neon (bokeh) and Crimson Moon (shooting stars). From a terminal: plasmify set shimmer petals (see Command line).

Animated GIF and WebP

Animated GIF and animated WebP pictures work as wallpapers: add them like any other picture, and Plasma plays them. A picture may be at most 8192 pixels on a side and have at most 1500 frames (a minute at 25 frames per second); bigger ones are refused before they are opened, so a broken or hostile file cannot eat your memory. The splash screen and the login screen only show still pictures, so they get a PNG of the first frame.

Credits for the artists

The wallpapers that come with a preset are downloaded from where they are published when you apply, never re-hosted. Many come from community collections that do not always know the original artist. Press Credits at the bottom of the main window to see, for each one, the artist when it is known, the licence when it is known, and where it comes from. Pictures you added yourself are credited as My own wallpaper.

If you are an artist and want an image credited differently or removed, please open an issue on the project's page.

When you share

When you share a preset, your own pictures travel inside the .plasmify file. Wallpapers by other artists are never copied: the file keeps their original address, and your friend's Plasmify downloads them from there. Only share pictures you are allowed to share.

Where the files are

PathWhat is in it
~/.local/share/plasmify/custom/<preset>/Pictures you added in the Customize tab
~/.local/share/plasmify/wallpapers/<preset>/The wallpapers of an applied preset (downloads are kept, so applying again does not fetch them again)
~/.local/share/plasmify/wallpapers/<preset>/living/The living Shimmer versions of the first six pictures (and the rest of the slideshow, still), which the slideshow plays while Shimmer is on

Downloads of presets you no longer use can be deleted with File > Clean up unused downloads or plasmify clean.
