Dark and light

Like your theme but want it brighter during the day? One click turns it into a light version of the same colours, and back.

The Dark / Light switch

In the header of the main window, next to the preset's name, there is a one-click switch. It says which way it goes: Switch to dark or Switch to light. Click it to flip the theme that is on your desktop right now (the preset marked ● Active, no matter which preset you are looking at) between a dark and a light version. Hover over it to see what it will do.

The switch is greyed out until you have applied a theme with Plasmify.

What happens when you switch:

  • The same colours, the other way round. Backgrounds and text are rebuilt for the new mode from the theme's own hue, with good contrast between them, so text stays easy to read. Your accent stays: it keeps its colour and is only made lighter or darker where that is needed to stay readable. The terminal colours are treated the same way.
  • Matching icons. Papirus icons switch to the matching variant (Papirus-Dark for dark, Papirus for light), and Breeze icons switch between Breeze and Breeze Dark. Other icon themes stay as they are.
  • A matching application style. A Kvantum theme paints its widgets for one mode only, so while the theme is in the other mode, apps use the Breeze application style, which follows the new colours. With glass on, glass stays: apps use a glass Kvantum theme coloured from the new palette instead. Its see-through amount follows the new mode (light palettes are a little less see-through) unless you chose one yourself. Switching back brings the preset's Kvantum theme back.
  • Only colour-related parts change. Your wallpapers, panel layout, fonts, cursor and effects stay as they are. The colour scheme is updated, and so are the other parts that depend on colour (application style, icons, panel style, terminal, prompt and splash screen), but only those you applied for this theme before: a part you left unticked stays untouched.
  • Right away. The change is applied immediately; you do not need to press Apply theme. The usual progress window appears, a backup is taken first, and only the colour-related parts run, so it is quick. Plasmify's own window follows at once. Other apps that are open follow too. With glass, apps that are already open keep their old look until you restart them: the style stays Kvantum in both modes, and Kvantum reads its theme only when an app starts. Terminal windows that are already open may keep their old colours; new ones get the new colours.
  • Remembered. The new colours are kept as tweaks of that preset, so the Customize tab shows them and the next Apply keeps the mode. Changes you had not applied yet are kept, except colours and the icon theme, which take the new mode's.

It works for any preset: the built-in ones, presets you imported, and presets you made in the theme studio or saved from Customize.

From a terminal, plasmify theme --light, plasmify theme --dark and plasmify theme --toggle do the same (see Command line).

Switching back

Click the switch again to return to the other mode. Going back to the mode the preset was designed in brings back exactly the colours you had before: the preset's own palette, or your own accent and background tweaks. If you changed colours in Customize while in the other mode, you get the preset's palette with your accent.

Undo

Like an apply, a switch backs up your settings first, so Restore… (or File > Restore a backup…) puts them back. After a restore (or after applying a preset from the command line), the switch and the Customize tab follow the colours on your desktop. See Backups and restore.

Related

  • To choose between dark and light when you create a theme from a picture, use the Colours step of the theme studio.
  • To change individual colours of a preset, use All colours… in the Customize tab.
