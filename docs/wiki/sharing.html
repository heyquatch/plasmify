Sharing presets

Keep the look you built, send it to a friend as one file, or turn the desktop you already have into a preset.

Save as my preset

Save as my preset… (at the bottom of the main window) saves the selected preset, including everything you changed in the Customize tab, as a preset of your own. Give it a name (up to 60 characters). It appears in the gallery with (yours) in front of its name, and your own pictures are stored with it.

Share

Share… (or File > Share selected preset…, Ctrl+S) writes the selected preset to a single .plasmify file. Send it to anyone; they open it with File > Import preset….

  • Your own pictures travel inside the file: wallpapers you added, your terminal photo, and your splash and login images.
  • Other artists' wallpapers stay linked to their source. They are never copied into the file; the file keeps their original address, and your friend's Plasmify downloads them from there when they apply. The artists keep their credit.

A .plasmify file is a zip archive holding a preset.toml and an assets folder with the pictures. From the command line: plasmify export <preset>.

To share your look with everyone, post it with a screenshot in the Presets category of Discussions, where you can also find presets other people made. GitHub only accepts .zip uploads, so rename the file first, for example my-desktop.plasmify to my-desktop.plasmify.zip. Your own pictures travel inside the file, so post it only if they are yours to share and you are happy for them to be public.

Import

File > Import preset… (Ctrl+O) adds a .plasmify file (also when renamed to .zip) or a plain .toml preset to your gallery. Afterwards Plasmify tells you how many pictures of its own it contains, which servers it downloads from when applied, and whether you will be asked before an untrusted server is contacted. If you already have a preset with the same name, you are asked whether to replace it.

Importing never applies anything: look at the preset, check the preview and Credits, and apply it when you are ready. A preset that needs its own pictures must be imported as the .plasmify file, not as the bare .toml. You can also drop .toml presets into ~/.config/plasmify/presets (File > Open presets folder). Command line: plasmify import <file>.

Save current desktop as preset

File > Save current desktop as preset… turns the look you built by hand into a preset. Colours, application style (with the glass look and how see-through it is), fonts, panels, the panel style Plasmify made for them, window effects (every setting of the Windows section in Customize), terminal and wallpapers are read from your current setup, so applying the saved preset brings this look back (apart from the parts listed below). Your exact panels are captured from the running Plasma shell, including the order of the widgets.

  • Give it a name and, if you like, a description.
  • Include my wallpapers copies your wallpaper pictures into the preset. Only share pictures you are allowed to share.
  • Parts that others could not install automatically are left out, and you are told which: for example an icon theme or cursor Plasmify cannot download, widgets it does not know, or fonts others would have to install themselves. A Plasma style of your own is not part of a preset either: others keep theirs.

Command line: plasmify save-desktop "My Desktop".

Untrusted hosts

A preset can list files to download. Plasmify contacts GitHub, Codeberg and GitLab without asking, and the built-in presets only use those. If a shared preset wants to download from any other server, Plasmify lists those servers and asks before contacting them, because a server learns the IP address of everyone who downloads from it. Only continue if you trust whoever shared the preset.

  • In the window, a dialog lists the servers when you press Apply theme.
  • On the command line, Plasmify asks in the terminal, or you approve each server with --allow-host HOST. --yes never approves them.
  • Downloads from servers you did not approve are refused, and so are redirects to such servers or to plain HTTP. Git downloads follow no redirects at all.
  • Server names must be written in plain letters a-z, digits, dots and dashes. A look-alike such as gіthub.com with a Cyrillic letter is refused, so it can never be listed as if it were GitHub. International names are listed in their xn-- form.

Privacy notes

  • Nothing is uploaded. Share and Export only write a file on your computer; you decide where it goes.
  • Pictures are copied as they are, including any information your camera or phone stored in them (such as the place a photo was taken). Remove it first if that matters to you.
  • The terminal greeting hides user@host unless you switch it on, so screenshots of your themed terminal do not reveal your user and computer name.
  • What a shared preset can and cannot do on your computer is summarised in Security and privacy.

Managing your presets

  • Your presets live in ~/.config/plasmify/presets: one .toml file each, plus a folder with its pictures.
  • Delete (shown for your own presets only) removes a preset and its pictures from your gallery, together with its remembered tweaks.
  • Built-in presets cannot be deleted or replaced: a preset of yours can never take the id (the short name, such as nord-minimal) of a built-in one.
  • To write a preset by hand, see the preset reference and check it with plasmify validate my-preset.toml.
